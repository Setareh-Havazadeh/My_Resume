Hi — I’m Your Name, and this is where your story goes. Not a résumé—a narrative. I don’t just list what I’ve done; I tell you why it mattered—and, full disclosure, I do it with a deeply suspicious number of em-dashes.

Here’s the thing—the em-dash isn’t punctuation, it’s a philosophy. It’s not a pause; it’s a held breath—the dramatic little beat right before the part that actually matters. Replace this paragraph with your own bona fides—but, if I may—keep at least one em-dash. For me.

These are placeholder words—known affectionately as Em-Dash Ipsum—and they exist for exactly one reason: to show you the shape of a paragraph before you have anything to say. It’s not lorem ipsum—it’s lorem ipsum that won’t shut up about typography. (There’s a working generator in the AI Lab, naturally.)

Away from the keyboard? That’s the Personal section’s job. The point of this template isn’t to impress you with my words—it’s to hand you a structure so good that your words land. Delete mine. Keep the dashes. You know the drill.
